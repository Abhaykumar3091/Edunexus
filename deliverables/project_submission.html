Project Information

Project Title: UniAssist AI
Multi-Agent University Student Support Platform

PPT with Brief Description:

https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1XhnknVW1iRVzSnQEdfwAp4LUgHt9r-Jo/edit?slide=id.p1#slide=id.p1

Technology Stack

  • Azure AI Foundry
  • GPT-4o-mini
  • FastAPI
  • Multi-Agent AI Architecture
  • Python
  • Web Technologies

Objective

To provide students with a persistent, intelligent university support platform where they can query academic policies with zero-hallucination grounded citations, track attendance and fees, access exam schedules, and resolve institutional grievances through autonomous AI agents.

1. Working Prototype / PoC

Project: UniAssist AI — Multi-Agent University Student Support Platform

Prototype PPT:

https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1XhnknVW1iRVzSnQEdfwAp4LUgHt9r-Jo/edit?slide=id.p1#slide=id.p1

Brief description:

An interactive AI-powered academic and student support platform that allows students to query university regulations, inspect real-time attendance and fee records, view examination schedules, and file institutional grievances with automated ticket tracking through a multi-agent simulation.
